# CONTENT.md — DK LAO Copywriting, SEO, and Bilingual Lao/English Rules

## 1. Purpose

This file controls customer-facing copy, SEO metadata, product descriptions, CTAs, bilingual structure, and content quality.

Content must support DK LAO as a trusted industrial equipment partner and must convert visitors into qualified leads.

## 2. Voice and Tone

Use a professional B2B tone:

- Clear
- Practical
- Commercial
- Trust-building
- Operationally focused
- Technical when needed

Avoid unsupported superlatives, hype, casual slang, and vague brochure copy.

## 3. Approved Core Message

```txt
DK LAO — Lifting, Moving, Cleaning & Growing Your Business
```

## 4. Business Positioning

DK LAO should be positioned as a one-stop industrial equipment partner in Laos for:

```txt
Material handling equipment
Forklift rental
New and used forklift sales
Forklift maintenance
Spare parts and accessories
Warehouse equipment and racking
Professional cleaning equipment
After-sales service
```

## 7. Bilingual Lao/English Rules

- English may be primary for corporate/technical content.
- Lao should be used for customer accessibility where required.
- Do not mix Lao and English in the same sentence unless it is a product/category label.
- Use clear Lao labels for forms, CTAs, and service categories when localization is implemented.
- Keep bilingual labels consistent across pages.

## 8. Claim Control

Do not write:

```txt
Best in Laos
Cheapest price
Guaranteed lowest price
Always available
Official distributor
Certified by [brand]
```

unless verified and approved.

Acceptable wording:

```txt
Reliable industrial equipment solutions
Flexible rental options
Professional maintenance support
Selected used equipment
Quotation-based pricing
Availability on request
```

## 9. Content QA

Before publishing, confirm:

- Copy supports a business action.
- No fake price or stock claim is included.
- CTA is visible.
- SEO title exists.
- Product/service category is clear.
- Lao/English terms are consistent.
